# Learnings

- A review finding is a claim to check at the source, not an instruction: two of four held word for word, one did not exist in the file.
- AICEP's Portugal Global pages are short and dated; an announcement of an event is past tense once the date has gone. Check the event date against today's date before writing a verb.
- Two sources may disagree (Fortune: suppliers to H&M are launch customers; The Next Web: H&M is one of the first customers). Say both rather than choosing.
- The gate's title limit is under 12 words; titles are checked.
- release.sh rebases on dev and the curator and others push often; expect a categories.json conflict and take theirs first.
- Fetching a source page: `curl -sL -A "Mozilla/5.0"` works through the proxy for portugalglobal.pt, fortune.com and thenextweb.com.
