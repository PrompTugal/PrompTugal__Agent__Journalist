## Figures confirmed on the page (first wave of a survey Algarve Evolution repeats every six months)

- 89 businesses (one business that answered twice counted once); data collected 7 October to 12 November 2026; responses confidential, analysed in aggregate.
- 56 of 89 (63%) use AI regularly or have it built in: 27 built into processes, products or services, 29 regular for some tasks. 20 (22%) only some staff, on their own initiative; 13 (15%) do not use it or considered it and did not go ahead.
- Accommodation (35 businesses, 39% of the sample): 43% use AI regularly or built in, against 63% in other sectors and 100% of the 19 in consulting and technology.
- 82% say their team is receptive; 75% say management knows where AI can help; 54% have rules on what may be put into AI tools; 38% have a budget set aside for the next 12 months.
- 56 businesses (63%) want help; of them 57% want practical team training, 52% an individual diagnosis, 41% a pilot project with UAlg, 36% help with applications.

## Caveat that must travel with any use (the page's own)

Respondents chose to take part, so the sample does not represent all Algarve businesses. 74% have fewer than 10 people and 39% are in accommodation. Those who answer a survey on AI tend to already be interested in it, so adoption is probably higher than the regional average. Groups compared have 11 to 66 businesses, so differences of a few points are not reliable.
